I’m Jason Deutsch, a Staff Software Engineer at Upwork. I work on applied AI infrastructure, including agent memory, orchestration, and the services that connect models to production systems.

My background spans distributed systems, cloud platforms, and engineering leadership. Before Upwork, I built cloud services at Magic Leap, led microservice development at IBM, and worked on backend systems at Outdoorsy. As a consulting Head of Engineering at Bigfoot, I built the initial platform and hired and led a team of seven engineers.

I studied Computer Science at the graduate level at Columbia University, focusing on Software Systems, and hold a BS in Computer Science from Southern New Hampshire University.

This notebook draws on that work: how to build reliable systems, make deliberate use of AI, and help engineering teams reason clearly about what they’re building.
